## 6. Performance Evaluation

The project successfully executed its configured dbt models and generated analytical outputs in BigQuery.

However, formal execution-time benchmarks, query latency measurements, and resource-utilization measurements were not collected.

Therefore, no specific runtime or speed improvement is claimed.

## 7. Accuracy and Limitations

- dbt tests validate configured data-quality conditions; they do not prove that every business metric is accurate.
- The CRM dataset is synthetic.
- The GA4 data comes from a public sample ecommerce dataset.
- Attribution models use rule-based assumptions rather than causal measurement.
- The time-decay attribution model uses an assumed seven-day half-life.
- Campaign spend values are illustrative and are not verified advertising expenditure.
- Customer lifetime value calculations use assumptions and a limited purchasing sample.
- No independent ground-truth dataset was available to calculate predictive accuracy.

## 8. Final Testing Summary

The project successfully completed the configured dbt debugging, model execution, and testing process.

The six dbt models support customer profiling, order analysis, session analysis, marketing touchpoint analysis, attribution, and campaign performance reporting.

The resulting data supports the Customer 360 and Campaign Performance dashboards published through Tableau Public.

Formal performance benchmarking and independent accuracy validation remain future improvements.
